## 2. Positioning

- **Niche:** male self-improvement ("lock in", discipline, NoFap, looksmaxxing-lite) for teens and young adults.
- **Sub-niche:** **a cartoon gorilla telling short behavioural-science stories about fixing your life.** Topics include dopamine, habit loops, reward-prediction error, circadian rhythm, cortisol, and testosterone.
- **Category:** *educational-comedy with a motivational close.* It is more earnest than A. Videos end on a call to change your life, not on a punchline.
- **Repeated subjects:** porn/NoFap/gooning; testosterone and looks (skin, height, cortisol "uglier"); daily-habit routines (sleep, mornings, energy); focus/study/procrastination (18 videos, the largest pillar); life-direction/mindset ("wasting your youth", "self-educated", "unf*ck your life").
- **Central promise:** *"Monke teach you to become better monke."* In practice: understand *why* your brain sabotages you, then use one system to fix it. The app is presented as that system.
- **Why choose B:**
  - It talks to the viewer's *life* (exams, parents, mornings, porn, purpose), not only the gym.
  - Its scripts are more sophisticated (Sun Tzu, rat/cocaine experiments, reward-prediction error) while staying silly.
  - It ships weekly.
- **Consistency:** high, but the voice has evolved (see §3).

## 5. Video construction (summary — autopsies in [05](05-storytelling-editing.md))

**Formula (Phase 2; the story cold open appears in 11 of 13 transcribed 2026 videos — the exceptions are the list videos "5 habits for great mornings" and "30 small habits"):**
1. **Story cold open (0–30 s):** Monkey in a relatable failure scene ("Monkey wake up stressed. He got exams coming up way too fast…"; "Monkey got that post nut clarity…"; "Monkey in the supermarket…").
2. **Stakes and promise:** "Luckily for Monkey… So today, Monkey teach you…" (lands 0:20–0:50).
3. **Mechanism sections**, often numbered steps, each built on a mini-story or experiment.
4. **App integration**: either a narrated plug (2:40–3:10 in "Self-educated"; 2:50–3:10 in "Morning routine") or **on-screen "Track this habit in the Monke App" cards** repeated through the video (2:06, 3:33–3:43, 4:56 in "Quit gooning").
5. **Emotional close + "Stay Monke."**

- **Pacing [Measured]:** median **164.5 WPM** (range 127–201). On 2-s-sampled 2026 videos the image changes on 79–91% of frames, an average hold of **≈ 2.2–2.5 s**, faster than A's ≈ 2.7–3.1 s and faster than B's own Phase-1 videos (≈ 3.4–3.9 s).

**Reading [Interpretation]:** B wins with **identity-level, emotionally loaded framing** ("You're probably wasting your youth.", "NoFap is BS. Do this instead.", "How to become dangerously self-educated."). It loses with **tactical productivity** ("7 habits that make you so productive it's unfair": 19K) and **gym content**, which is A's turf ("If you gym but are still skinny…": 13K; "Every muscle-growth cheat code…": 17K).

### Formats
1. **"N habits…"** (5 habits for good sleep / clean, glassy skin / great mornings / insane ooga booga energy / that will make you more attractive; 30 small habits; 7 habits… productive): the most reliable format. Median 279K, 4 of 7 at or above 279K. The one productivity entry is the outlier at 19K.
2. **Contrarian reframe:** "NoFap is BS. Do this instead." / "Don't delete social media. Do this instead." Both 600K+.
3. **Identity statement:** "You're probably wasting your youth." / "High testosterone will solve all your problems." Short declarative titles, often ending in a full stop.
4. **Time-boxed promise:** "How to quit gooning forever in 5 mins." / "Give monke 5mins…" (mixed: 1.43M vs 41K).
5. **Phase-1 legacy:** "How To [X] Explained By Monke": a straight copy of A's template (Grow Taller 1.0M; Focus 108K).
6. **Shorts:** vertical cream panels with a caption and "(explained by monke)". Mostly repurposed slides. Median **5.7K** (max 39K).

### Title system (shift over time) [Measured]
- Avg 45 characters. 14/50 start "How to". 14/50 contain a number.
- **16 titles end with a full stop**, 15 of them in 2026. Sentence case replaced Title Case. Deliberately understated, "lowercase-confident" packaging: "How to become dangerously self-educated."
- Titles avoid the word "monke" after 2025, except "30 small habits that quietly change a monke's life." and "Give monke 5mins…".

## 10. Strengths, weaknesses, risks [Interpretation]

| Strengths | Weaknesses / risks |
|---|---|
| Story-led cold opens that make the viewer the protagonist | The visual identity is close to A's, so B is easy to confuse with A and hard to defend as its own |
| Weekly cadence plus a growing app/community funnel. The video *is* the ad, so no third-party sponsor fatigue | Re-uses scripts (its own testosterone opener three times; an A-matching testosterone script) |
| Range: can cover any life topic, not only fitness | The productivity pillar is crowded and weak (median 25K over 18 videos) |
| More sophisticated scripting (theories, experiments, quotes) | Adult/drug humour limits brand-safe sponsorship and may age-gate |
| Contrarian, identity-level titles that travel on browse/suggested | Shorts are almost dead weight (median 5.7K) |
