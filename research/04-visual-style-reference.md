## Shared DNA (true of both channels)

Both channels draw **the same character archetype in the same style**: a **hand-drawn, flat-colour, heavily outlined brown gorilla with a pale beige face mask and two small "dot" eyes (white circles with black pupils)**. Muscle anatomy is suggested by a few interior strokes. A viewer scrolling past would struggle to tell the channels apart without reading the background colour (A: white, B: cream) or the type style. Everything below describes how each channel works within that shared DNA.

### A2. Shape language
- **Dominant shapes:** large **rounded masses** (deltoids, biceps, traps) stacked into a top-heavy triangle. Rounded forms keep a "huge" body friendly and comic.
- **Contrast shapes:** angular, geometric elements are reserved for *information*: hexagonal steroid-ring molecules, bar charts, tier-list rows, red straight arrows.
- **Shape jokes:** banana (penis), coconuts (pecs / shoulders / testicles), deflating or wilting banana (porn, low T).

### A3. Linework
- **Black outlines.** Measured median dark-run thickness: **~2.1% of image width** on the 1634-px avatar (≈34 px), ≈ 15 px on a 1280-px thumbnail. Thick enough to survive the 168-px-wide mobile thumbnail.
- **Hand-drawn digital marker:** rounded caps, slight wobble, line weight varies 25–30% (IQR 29–39 px on the avatar). Some joins are left open.
- Few interior lines: only muscle ticks and face-mask edge. **Clarity first.**

**Temperature / harmony:** warm brown character on a neutral white field, with one warm accent (red) and occasional cool accents (cyan drool, green check). Low saturation overall; the colour comes from props.

### A5. Brightness & contrast [Measured]
- Mean frame luminance **≈ 182–210 / 255** (high-key). Dark frames (luma < 90) make up 0–9% of a video, mostly the night-bedroom scenes in "Gooning".
- **Figure–ground separation is extreme:** a dark-brown, black-outlined figure on near-white. No gradients, glows or drop shadows in the core style. The exceptions are a few thumbnails (HGH: neon glow on black; SCIENCE: chrome gradient text on black).
- **Readability at mobile size:** excellent for the white thumbnails. Five thumbnails use off-signature backgrounds (black, grey, a pink/green split, or a dark photo). Three of them sit below the channel median: 10% Bodyfat 56K, SCIENCE 30K, Belly Fat 11K. HGH (141K) sits just above it. "Best Foods" (752K) is the clear exception: dark, but carried by a bright, colour-coded tier list.

### A6. Backgrounds & environments
- **Default:** a white void. The character and handwritten text carry the frame.
- **Recurring sets:** a bedroom (bed, teal-grey blanket, poster, window, a night variant), gym equipment drawn as plain line art, anatomical inserts (pink brain with green hypothalamus; fat-cell blobs; testicle cross-sections), simple graphs (red curve on white axes).
- No parallax or layered depth observed. Background effort is minimal. **Production effort goes into props and diagrams.**

**Where the effort goes [Interpretation]:** custom diagrams and props per video. Character poses are a re-used library (the presenter lean, flex, thumbs-up, lying in bed, holding a phone).

### A8. Timing [Measured / Estimate]
- **Average hold per visual state:** **≈ 2.7–3.1 s** (2-s-sampled videos; the visual changes on 64–73% of consecutive samples). The 1-s-sampled "Skinny Fat" gives ≈ 2.3 s, consistent with its 190 WPM.
- **Skit beats:** the "Monkey Soy" scam skit runs ~12 s (0:11–0:23); the testicle "make the juice" dialogue gag runs ~15 s (2:50–3:05).
- **Section header cards** hold ~2–4 s (e.g. "Get Lean But Not Shredded", 1:32–1:34).

### A9. Editing & transitions
Hard cuts dominate. No wipes or flashy transitions observed. Zoom-ins act as transitions into detail. Section breaks are marked by **text title cards with small banana-sparkle icons**, not by transitions.

### A10. On-screen text
- **Font:** casual handwritten marker (black). The thumbnails use the same lettering at a large size. One recent exception ("HOLLOW CHEEKS") uses a bold condensed all-caps sans.
- **Use:** (1) section titles, (2) key nouns as they are spoken ("Aromatase", "Cortisol", "5 hours", "7–9 hours"), (3) bullet lists that build ("How Cortisol Stay High • Poor sleep • Overtraining • Work / school anxiety"), (4) small labels on diagrams.
- **Comic text:** short labels on gags ("Monkey Soy", "Quick over here", "Slap!", "She wants my…").
- No subtitles or captions burned in.
