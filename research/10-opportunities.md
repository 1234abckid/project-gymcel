# Deliverable 10 — Opportunity Analysis & Video Recommendations

> **Scope reminder:** this is an evidence-ranked list of *content opportunities*, not a channel design. Proposed titles are working examples to make each opportunity concrete. "The mascot" means any original character to be designed later. These recommendations deliberately avoid the gorilla archetype already used by A, B and at least one other channel ([07 §4](07-niche-trend-report.md#4-competitive-landscape)).

## 1. Ranking method

Each opportunity is scored 1–5 on six criteria (5 = most favourable):

| Criterion | 5 means… | Main evidence used |
|---|---|---|
| **Demand** | Strong, current search or recommendation pull | Autocomplete, the reference channels' own outliers, reporting |
| **Competition** | Few strong incumbents *in this format* | "Up next" snapshot, category map, A/B coverage |
| **Distinctiveness** | Hard to confuse with A or B | Gap analysis |
| **Ease** (inverse difficulty) | Cheap in the cutout style | [08](08-production-workflow.md) |
| **Repeatability** | Can become a series | Topic breadth |
| **Brand potential** | Builds a recognisable identity, not one-off views | [Interpretation] |

## 3. Video directions by category (ranked within each)

### Category A — Proven formats (demonstrated in the reference channels)

**A1. "N habits for [universal body outcome]" — sleep edition**
- **Concept:** 5 evidence-backed habits for deeper sleep, each a mini-scene.
- **Working title:** "5 habits that fix your sleep (that nobody does)"
- **Subject:** sleep · **Interest:** universal; students and gym-goers.
- **Why click:** proven outlier topic (B 1.8M); simple promise.
- **Hook:** the mascot "sleeping" like a corpse at 3 a.m. while doom-scrolling. The phone light is the only light in the frame.
- **Visual approach:** night palette; one prop per habit; a sleep-quality meter.
- **Format:** list, with a story frame (night 1 → night 7).
- **Traffic type:** search + recommendation.
- **Evidence:** B "5 habits for good sleep." 1.8M; "sleepmaxxing" completions.
- **Competition:** high on the topic, so the edge must come from execution.
- **Difficulty:** low.
- **Follow-ups:** sleepmaxxing tier list; caffeine timing; naps.

**A2. Contrarian reframe of a sacred cow**
- **Concept:** challenge a belief the audience holds, then offer a better practice.
- **Working title:** "Cold showers are overrated. Do this instead."
- **Subject:** discipline myths · **Interest:** lock-in culture.
- **Why click:** contrarian title pattern (B's "…Do this instead." median 613K).
- **Hook:** the mascot shivering under a cold shower, narrating its own heroics.
- **Visual approach:** split "myth / reality" panels.
- **Format:** claim → why people believe it → what the evidence says → the better habit.
- **Traffic type:** recommendation.
- **Evidence:** B's "NoFap is BS" 1.5M; "Don't delete social media" 613K.
- **Competition:** medium.
- **Difficulty:** low–medium.
- **Follow-ups:** "Waking up at 5 a.m. is overrated"; "Monk mode is making you weird" (already a typed completion).

**A3. Story-first taboo explainer**
- **Concept:** a specific, embarrassing moment opens the video; the explanation follows.
- **Working title:** "Why you feel worse after quitting [bad habit] (and why that's good)"
- **Subject:** withdrawal / the "pit of despair" · **Interest:** quitting porn, vaping or scrolling.
- **Why click:** emotional specificity.
- **Hook:** day 3 of quitting: the mascot is irritable, everything is annoying, its own thoughts heckle it.
- **Visual approach:** a mood-weather system (storm clouds clearing over days).
- **Format:** story → mechanism → protocol → emotional close.
- **Traffic type:** recommendation + search ("how to quit vaping" is a top completion).
- **Evidence:** B's Quit gooning 1.43M and its "pit of despair" section; autocomplete for "how to quit".
- **Competition:** medium.
- **Difficulty:** medium.
- **Follow-ups:** per-habit series (vaping, sugar, scrolling).

### Category B — Adaptable formats (proven, taken in a new direction)

**B1. Tier list: supplements ranked by evidence**
- **Concept:** rank 15 popular supplements S → F, using the mascot's reactions as the scoring device.
- **Working title:** "I ranked every gym supplement by actual science"
- **Subject:** creatine, whey, ashwagandha, testosterone boosters, etc. · **Interest:** gym beginners.
- **Why click:** curiosity and validation ("is my supplement trash?").
- **Hook:** the mascot drinks a "testosterone booster" and nothing happens.
- **Visual approach:** a coloured tier board. Each item drops in with a one-line verdict. On-screen source cards.
- **Format:** ranking.
- **Traffic type:** search + recommendation.
- **Evidence:** A's "Best Foods" tier list 752K (A's best recent); trainer winny tier list 1.6M; "creatine explained by ape" searches.
- **Competition:** medium (fitness creators), low in mascot form.
- **Difficulty:** medium (research).
- **Follow-ups:** sleep-aid tier list, study-method tier list, skincare tier list.

**B2. Creatine myth-buster**
- **Working title:** "Does creatine make you bald? (the truth, explained simply)"
- **Interest:** very high-intent search ("creatine hair loss").
- **Hook:** the mascot nervously counting its hair before every scoop.
- **Visual approach:** "myth / evidence" scale; a cartoon study card.
- **Format:** question → myth origin → evidence → verdict.
- **Traffic type:** search.
- **Evidence:** autocomplete; no mascot-format competitor found.
- **Difficulty:** low–medium.
- **Follow-ups:** creatine for women; loading phase; creatine in coffee.

**B3. Charisma through a story**
- **Working title:** "How to become someone everyone loves talking to"
- **Interest:** the #1 typed completion of "how to become".
- **Hook:** the mascot at a party, mid-awkward-silence, rehearsing lines in its head (shown as thought bubbles).
- **Visual approach:** a conversation "health bar"; replays of the same scene done right.
- **Format:** before → 3 skills → after replay.
- **Traffic type:** search + recommendation.
- **Evidence:** B's charisma video 194K in ~6 days; ThatGuy 2.7M in the "Up next" list.
- **Competition:** medium.
- **Difficulty:** medium.
- **Follow-ups:** small talk, eye contact, being funny, texting.

**B4. Debunk the trend your audience is already watching**
- **Working title:** "Mewing, bone smashing, hollow cheeks: what actually works?"
- **Interest:** looksmaxxing curiosity plus safety worry.
- **Why click:** news-level awareness plus forbidden-knowledge appeal.
- **Hook:** the mascot about to hit its face with a hammer; a second character stops it.
- **Visual approach:** a "real / fake / dangerous" stamp system.
- **Format:** myth-by-myth verdicts.
- **Traffic type:** trend + search.
- **Evidence:** mainstream looksmaxxing coverage and backlash ([07 §1](07-niche-trend-report.md)); A's "Hollow Cheeks" upload shows the references moving there.
- **Competition:** medium (doctors, commentators), low in comedy animation.
- **Difficulty:** medium (accuracy burden).
- **Follow-ups:** "10x testosterone?" fact-check; jawline exercises; posture.

**D1. Women's glow-up / confidence explainers**

- **Evidence:** "self improvement for women", "glow up tips for girls", "creatine for women", "how to increase testosterone in women" completions. A and B never address women.
- **Hook / visual:** requires a distinct mascot and tone, not a re-skin of the male format.
- **Traffic type:** search + recommendation.
- **Competition:** unverified in animated form (needs a manual YouTube check).
- **Difficulty:** medium.
- **Follow-ups:** a series.

**D2. Faith-respectful self-control**
- **Working title:** "How to quit lust without hating yourself"
- **Evidence:** "how to stop gooning in christianity / islam / as a muslim", "how to quit lust", "how to lock in with god".
- **Approach:** compassionate, non-denominational framing, possibly with faith-specific episodes developed with community advisers.
- **Traffic type:** search.
- **Risk:** high sensitivity.
- **Difficulty:** medium–high.

**D3. Native animated Shorts series**
- **Working title (series):** "One myth in 30 seconds"
- **Evidence:** B's 18 Shorts (median 5.7K) are repurposed vertical slides with low visual change (29–73% of samples changing). Nobody in this set has made Shorts that are animated for vertical first.
- **Traffic type:** recommendation (Shorts feed), feeding long-form.
- **Difficulty:** medium (volume).

**D4. "Explained with sources" credibility layer** *(a cross-cutting device, not a single video)*
- On-screen citation cards and a pinned source list.
- Neither reference does this. It turns myth-busting into a moat and reduces the health-claim risk with a young audience.
