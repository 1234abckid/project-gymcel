## 1. The macro trend: "-maxxing" self-optimisation went mainstream in 2026 [Verified via reporting]

- **Dictionary.com named "maxxing" its 2026 Word of the Year**, citing looksmaxxing, sleepmaxxing and personalitymaxxing, and noting looksmaxxing's early-2026 attention spike ([WDBO / Cox](https://www.wdbo.com/news/trending/dictionarycom-selects-maxxing-2026-word-year/VEJDN4C3PBC3ZKN5MKBLJGNGIQ/?outputType=amp), [KATV](https://katv.com/news/nation-world/maxxing-named-dictionarycom-2026-word-of-the-year-why-internet-slang-maximize-looksmaxxing-tanmaxxing-nonnamaxxing-cozymaxxing-social-media-affordability-data-center-odyssey-unc-definition-viral)).
- **Looksmaxxing broke into mainstream news coverage** in late 2025 and early 2026, framed around teenage boys and health risks: bone-smashing, steroids, "mogging" ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/video/132337726/), [InDaily](https://www.indailyqld.com.au/news/in-depth/2026/04/17/what-is-looksmaxxing), [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/inside-looksmaxxing-the-viral-trend-pushing-men-to-hammer-their-own-faces)). Coverage reported search-interest jumps of several hundred percent early in 2026 ([UNILAD Tech](https://www.uniladtech.com/news/tech-news/looksmaxxing-doctor-warning-trend-searches-surge-376-575457-20260327)). Those are their figures; I could not verify them in Trends.
- **Backlash and counter-movement:** commentators pushed "charactermaxxing" as an alternative ([Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/media/tpusa-andrew-kolvet-calls-viral-looksmaxxing-craze-red-warning-sign-young-men.amp)). Its most visible figure, Clavicular, had YouTube channels banned and faces criminal charges (allegations, not convictions) ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/24/have-you-heard-clavicular-igumdrop-master-chef-me-at-the-zoo/amp/)).
- **Adjacent meme formats:** "Lookism edits" grew from Dec 2025 into 2026 ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/lookism-edits)).

**What this means for these channels [Interpretation]:** both sit next to the maxxing wave without being looksmaxxing channels. B even titles videos "Height Maxxing", "ENERGY-MAXXING", "Focusmaxxing". The trend supplies vocabulary and curiosity. The backlash creates room for a *safer, funnier, evidence-checked* voice.
