## 7. Engagement as a reach signal [Measured → Interpretation]

On both channels, **the bigger a new video's reach, the lower its like rate**:
- **A:** Best Foods 0.76% · Coconuts 1.8% · 10x T 2.3% · Hollow Cheeks 2.6%, versus 5.3–8.0% on its older and smaller uploads.
- **B:** Self-educated 1.4% · 30 habits 2.3% · Unf*ck 2.8% · Aura 2.9% · Quit gooning 3.0%, versus 5.7–7.8% on small 2025–early-2026 uploads.

**Reading:** YouTube is showing recent uploads to large non-subscriber audiences, who watch but rarely like, more than to the core fanbase. That fits the steep views-per-sub ratios on the breakouts. It is a proxy, not proof of traffic source.
